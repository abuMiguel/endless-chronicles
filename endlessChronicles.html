Controls

Desktop: W,A,S,D or Arrow Keys to move. SPACE = Melee. E = Ranged attack.

Mobile: D-Pad to move. ATK = Melee, E button = Ranged.

Knight: arrows (limited). Beast: rocks (limited). Wizard: fireball (costs mana). Pick up ammo from the ground!

Your character automatically attacks enemies that are very close, but manual attacking deals more damage or uses abilities.
