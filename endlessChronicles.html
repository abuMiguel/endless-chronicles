Lore of Grumbleshire

Origins: The Realm of Grumbleshire was created when the Great Wizard Wobblethwaite accidentally sneezed mid-spell, scattering arcane energy across the void. The resulting world was mostly fine, except for the persistent smell of cheese and the mysterious continent-sized crater to the east.

The Problem: Long ago, the legendary hero Bram the Adequate defeated the Dark Lord Snorflax the Mildly Inconvenient. Unfortunately, Bram forgot to actually finish the job — he got distracted by a sandwich — and Snorflax's descendants now run increasingly petty evil operations across the realm.

The Prophecy: The Oracle of Snorfburg (a surprisingly large frog named Gerald) prophesied: "One of slightly above-average combat skills shall traverse the realm, open many chests, and eventually reach The Void of Questionable Decisions, where Grand Witch Snorflaxia the Chronically Disappointed awaits." Gerald then went back to eating flies.

The Towns: Flumpton is where most heroes begin their journey, mostly because it's the only place with a decent inn. Bumblesnatch exists by accident. Sandpocket charges too much for water. Grimwhistle smells. Frostholm is very cold.

The Endgame: Deep in The Void lies the Citadel of Mediocre Doom. Snorflaxia awaits. She has been waiting for quite some time and is, as her name suggests, quite disappointed. Her minions are numerous and very unpleasant. Good luck. You'll need it.
